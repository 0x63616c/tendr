Tendr is a personal journal for recording medication doses (in mg) and body weight. There is no login, account or server: all data is stored locally on the device and nothing is transmitted.

How to review
1. On first launch, read the "A journal, not medical advice" notice and tap "I understand".
2. On Home, tap "Log dose" to record a dose in mg. On Progress, add a weight and set a goal. Every entry appears in the Journal tab.
3. Settings > Connect Apple Health requests read-only access to body weight. Tendr never writes to Apple Health and uses the weight only to show progress inside the app. Apple Health is optional; every feature works with manual entries.
4. Treatment > Your schedule sets dose days. Reminders are optional local notifications.

Medical context
Tendr is a record-keeping tool, not a dosing calculator. It never recommends a dose or a change to treatment. The "Estimated level" card is a simplified half-life curve drawn from the user's own logged doses. The app labels it as an estimate, not a measured level and not medical advice, and tells users to follow their prescriber. No drug brands are named and no medical claims are made.
